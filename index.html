Technology

Signal intelligence for operators who cannot afford gaps.

Orbint delivers the full stack, from space-qualified RF receivers to the analyst interface. No data leaves European infrastructure.

  • 01

    Electromagnetic order of battle

    Create and update a near real-time map of radar systems, radio networks, and electronic jamming gear across restricted or hostile zones. Multi-satellite signal processing locates each transmitter with sub-kilometer precision, while onboard analysis identifies its specific modulation, pulse patterns, and timing variations.

  • 02

    Maritime surveillance

    Ships that go dark by disabling their AIS transponder still radiate. Orbint correlates radar emissions and communications signatures against characterized emitter behaviors to resolve position to sub-kilometer accuracy, fused with prior tracks and terrain models in near real time.

  • 03

    Crisis early warning

    A new emitter appearing overnight, a radar shifting frequency, a transmitter changing its pulse rhythm: these are the signals that precede action. Orbint flags the change the moment a satellite pass captures it, even before the situation on the ground becomes visible.

Technology

Signals intelligence for operators who cannot afford gaps.

Orbint delivers the full stack, from space-qualified RF receivers to the analyst interface. No data leaves European infrastructure.

Governmental

For armed forces, intelligence services and security agencies operating under a national or European mandate.

  • Electromagnetic order of battle
  • Radar and emitter identification
  • Maritime domain awareness
  • GNSS jamming and spoofing attribution
  • Crisis early warning

Civilian

For regulators, infrastructure operators and commercial analysts who need to know what is transmitting, and from where.

  • Satellite interference geolocation
  • Spectrum regulation and licence enforcement
  • GNSS interference mapping for aviation
  • Illegal fishing and dark vessel detection
  • Critical infrastructure protection

    Airbus, Rohde & Schwarz, constellr, Orbint and HPS Sign MoU for Sovereign Space-Based ISR

    Airbus Defence and Space has signed an MoU with Rohde & Schwarz, constellr, Orbint and HPS to develop a sovereign, end-to-end satellite-based Earth observation and ISR solution for Europe.
